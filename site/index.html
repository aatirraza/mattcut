MattCut

The Mattrics video editor

Timeline editing, text, effects, transitions, filters and captions, with one-click export. Runs fully on your own computer, no account or internet needed.

Download for Mac Version 0.1.0 · Apple Silicon & Intel · macOS 11 or later
Download for Windows Version 0.1.0 · Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Install on Mac

  1. Open the downloaded file

    Double-click MattCut-mac-universal.dmg in your Downloads folder.

  2. Drag MattCut into Applications

    Drop the MattCut icon on the Applications folder in the window that opens, then eject the disk.

  3. Allow it the first time

    Open MattCut from Applications. macOS will say it can't check the app for malicious software, because this early build isn't signed with an Apple certificate yet. Click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to MattCut, then confirm with your password. You only do this once.

No "Open Anyway" button? Open the Terminal app, paste this line and press Return, then open MattCut again:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MattCut.app

Install on Windows

  1. Unzip the download

    Right-click MattCut-windows-x64.zip in Downloads and choose Extract All. Move the MattCut folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents.

  2. Open MattCut.exe

    Double-click MattCut.exe in that folder. Right-click it and choose Show more options › Send to › Desktop for a shortcut.

  3. Allow it the first time

    If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This early build isn't signed with a certificate yet. You only do this once.

Good to know

Your projects
Saved in Movies › MattCut Projects on Mac, Videos › MattCut Projects on Windows.
Sharing
Send the project's .fcproj file together with its footage, or keep both on the shared drive.
Exports
Saved in MattCut Exports next to your projects folder, unless you pick another folder.
Updating
Download again from this page and replace the old app (Applications on Mac, the MattCut folder on Windows). Your projects aren't touched.
